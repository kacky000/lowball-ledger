How Lowball checks a deal

Every deal on this board was found and read on Black Friday, 27 November 2026, at the store’s own page or in a dated deals roundup that names the store. Each card says when the price was read (UTC) and where. The usual price is the one that page states. The verdict uses only what the source says about the price’s history and the size of the cut: we never invent a price history. Prices change fast on Black Friday, so check the store before you buy.
